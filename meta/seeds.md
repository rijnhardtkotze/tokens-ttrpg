# Seeds — Pre-Planted Plot Threads

Possibilities that activate when the player approaches them. They give the world depth without forcing a path.

## Format

```
### <Seed Title>
- **Trigger:** when/how this becomes active
- **Content:** what is the truth behind it
- **Status:** dormant | active | resolved
```

---

_Empty. Add seeds as the campaign develops. Keep them loose enough that you can adjust the truth based on player direction, but specific enough that you remember the hook._
