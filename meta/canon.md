## Meta-principles of narration

_Suggested defaults (delete or replace as you like):_

- No deus-ex-machina resolutions
- Memory fragments / clues surface organically (triggers: smells, places, words)
- Not every clue leads somewhere — but many do
- NPCs have their own agendas, not just quest-giver functions
- The character's past is revealed slowly, not in session 2
