# State of the Story

## Where we are

The campaign opens at [[loc-graywater-quay]] on the morning after the harbor's
salt-lantern went dark for the first time in living memory. Eska has three unread
tideglass lumps from the *Brinemark* in her pouch and a hole where one year of her
life should be.

## Open mysteries

- Why did the salt-lantern die, and why won't it relight?
- How is Eska alive when the quay's drowning-rolls say she isn't?
- What is in the three unread lumps of *Brinemark* glass?

## Last scene

None played yet. The GM's first turn is the opening scene.
