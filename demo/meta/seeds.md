# Seeds

Pre-planted threads. Hidden from the character; known to the table.

## The glass that shouldn't exist

- **Seed:** Among the *Brinemark* salvage is a piece of tideglass holding a memory
  belonging to someone still alive.
- **Trigger:** The character reads salvage glass from the wreck, or sells it to
  [[npc-brother-helst]].

## Finn's ledger

- **Seed:** [[npc-odd-finn]] keeps a tally of who he has ferried *out* to the fog
  line at night. The tally is longer than the list of the drowned.
- **Trigger:** The character asks Finn for a night crossing, or searches his skiff.
