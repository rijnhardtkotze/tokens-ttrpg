# Canon — The Salt-Lantern Coast

Non-negotiable world rules. The GM never contradicts this file; proposed changes go
through `meta/contradictions.md` and the player.

## Genre & tone

Low fantasy, coastal gothic. Fog, brine, rope, rust. Wonder is rare, quiet, and
usually costs something. Violence is short and ugly; talk is the main tool.
Melancholy but not hopeless — lamplight matters *because* the dark is real.

## The premise

The Salt-Lantern Coast is a string of fishing harbors on a cold sea that does not
give back what it takes — except memories. When someone drowns, their memories wash
ashore weeks later as **tideglass**: small lumps of cloudy green glass that, held to
the ear, replay a moment of the drowned life. Salvaging, trading, and reading
tideglass is the coast's economy, religion, and curse.

## Hard rules

1. **The sea keeps bodies, returns memories.** Tideglass is real, common knowledge,
   and every piece holds exactly one moment from one drowned person.
2. **Salt-lanterns hold the fog line.** Every harbor keeps one burning. What moves
   inside the deep fog is never shown clearly and never named in canon.
3. **Reading tideglass leaves a residue.** Listen too often and you start carrying
   fragments of the drowned: their hungers, their grudges, their unfinished errands.
4. **No resurrection.** The drowned do not come back. Anything that claims otherwise
   is lying about what it is.
5. **Magic is craft, not fireworks.** It looks like lanternwork, knots, salt-lines,
   and glass — slow, local, and priced.
