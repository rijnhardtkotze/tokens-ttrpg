# Odd Finn

## Quick Reference

- Ferryman of [[loc-graywater-quay]]; ageless in the way of weathered men, mute on Sundays by private vow
- Runs the only skiff to the outer stacks; refuses some passengers without explanation
- Has crossed the fog line at night and come back — repeatedly, which should be impossible
- Treats Eska with a deference that looks almost like recognition, or fear
- His skiff never carries a lantern

## Details

Finn was ferrying before anyone living can remember him starting. He takes payment
in glass but never listens to it; he nails the pieces under his gunwales, green side
down, dozens of them. Asked why, he says the sea likes to hear itself think.

He keeps a tally, knife-cut into the tiller, of his night crossings. He has never
shown it to anyone, and he started cutting it the year Eska drowned.
