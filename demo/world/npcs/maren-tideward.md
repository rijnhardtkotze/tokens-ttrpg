# Maren Tideward

## Quick Reference

- Keeper of the harbor salt-lantern at [[loc-graywater-quay]]; sixties, rope-gray braid, voice like a held breath
- The only person who didn't treat Eska's return as a scandal — she signed for her against the drowning-rolls
- Practical lanternwork witch: salt-lines, wick-craft, fog-reading
- Currently frightened and hiding it badly: the lantern did not burn down, it *refused*
- Offers salvage rights to the *Brinemark* to whoever finds out why

## Details

Maren has kept the lantern for thirty years and lit it from her mother's wick, as
her mother did before her. She knows the lantern is not just a lamp — the keepers'
private journals, which she has never shown anyone, describe it as one end of a
bargain. She does not know who holds the other end anymore. That is what scares
her.

Her debt to Eska is real and personal: the night Eska walked out of the surf nine
years after drowning, Maren recognized something in her face that she has never
explained — and signed the rolls to make her legally alive. She has never asked
for anything in return. Until now.
