# Brother Helst

## Quick Reference

- Priest of the Drowned Ledger, the coast's funerary-mercantile order; runs the tideglass exchange at [[loc-graywater-quay]]
- Buys, grades, and "files" memory-glass; officially, dangerous pieces are sealed away
- Soft-spoken, exact, always slightly damp; counts on his fingers when he thinks no one is watching
- Listens to far more glass than doctrine allows — and it is starting to show
- Pays best for *Brinemark* salvage, no questions asked, which is itself a question

## Details

Helst's order treats every drowned memory as an entry in a ledger that must one day
balance. What balancing means is doctrine he never explains to the laity. He has
been at the quay for six years — long enough to know whose glass keeps surfacing
and where, and to notice patterns the drowning-rolls don't account for. Eska Voss
is one of those patterns.

The residue is in him: other people's hungers surface at his table, other people's
grudges in his sermons. He knows it. He considers it the cost of a balanced ledger.
