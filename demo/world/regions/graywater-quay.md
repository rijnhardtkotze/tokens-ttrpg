# Graywater Quay

## Quick Reference

- Fishing-and-salvage harbor of maybe two hundred souls; everything smells of tar and kelp
- The harbor salt-lantern, kept by [[npc-maren-tideward]], holds the fog line offshore — it died last night
- Economy runs on tideglass: divers bring it up, [[npc-brother-helst]] grades and buys it
- [[npc-odd-finn]] runs the only ferry between the quay and the outer stacks
- The drowning-rolls in the lantern house list every soul the sea has kept — including Eska Voss, nine years ago

## Details

Graywater Quay grew around its lantern, not its harbor: the anchorage is poor, but
the lantern's fog line has held for six generations, and that safety — not the fish
— is why people stay. The town is stacked up the cliff in tiers of rope-lashed
timber, the lantern house at the top like a head on a spine.

The wreck of the *Brinemark*, a memory-trader that went down with a full hold two
months ago, lies just inside the fog line. Salvage rights are disputed; diving it
is tolerated, profitable, and quietly feared.

With the lantern dark, the fog has begun moving in for the first time in living
memory. Nobody has yet said aloud what that means.
